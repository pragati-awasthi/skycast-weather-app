SkyCast – Dynamic Weather Dashboard

SkyCast is a modern web-based weather dashboard that delivers real-time, location-specific weather updates in a clean and interactive interface. It integrates live data from trusted weather APIs to provide users with accurate temperature readings, weather conditions, and short-term forecasts.

The dashboard is designed with simplicity and usability in mind, ensuring that anyone can quickly check the weather without navigating complex menus. Users can view not just the current conditions but also forecasts for the coming days, helping them make better decisions for daily routines, commutes, and travel planning.

SkyCast also highlights additional details like wind speed, humidity, air quality, UV index, and sunrise/sunset times, giving a complete snapshot of the atmosphere. The design follows a glassmorphic style with smooth animations, making it both visually appealing and user-friendly across devices.

By combining functionality with aesthetics, SkyCast makes checking the weather an engaging experience, not just a necessity.
